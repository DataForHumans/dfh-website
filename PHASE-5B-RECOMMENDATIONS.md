# Phase 5B visual recommendations

These are proposals for the next design phase. Phase 5A changes the website’s copy and makes a small typographic adjustment for the new homepage headline.

- Look for documentary photographs that show cooperation in the ordinary course of community work. A planning session, volunteers sorting supplies together, or staff preparing for an event can convey the work through gestures and relationships. Use real settings with permission and check the frame for screens, names, forms, or other identifying details.
- Pair a human moment with analytical evidence where the relationship is clear. On Work, preserve the dashboard and chart at readable sizes. Add a photograph only where it offers context about how the client does the work.
- Develop the existing edge-cropped network illustrations as a visual expression of cooperation. Try a single ochre node as a recurring point of attention within a connected cluster. Use the device in a few intentional places and keep it outside the analytical graphics.
- Reserve deeper forest-green surfaces for a small number of moments, such as the closing invitation or footer. Keep the cream and sage editorial surfaces, serif headlines, and calm analytical treatment. Test the headline, controls, and reading order with any new artwork at narrow widths.
