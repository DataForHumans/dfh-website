Federal funding changes were coming, and each food-relief organization needed to know what they might mean for its own budget.

Instead of 100+ separate reports, we built one calculator: an organization enters its own funding data and sees its likely exposure in under five minutes.

The whole region got the same starting point for hard conversations.
