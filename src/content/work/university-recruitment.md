### The challenge

Advertising, email, texts and calls ran through several systems, each counting success its own way, and no single view showed which efforts led to enrollment.

### What we are working on

We are tracing each prospective student's path from first contact to enrollment and flagging what isn't tracked well enough to guide spending.
