### The challenge

Nearly a thousand volunteer records sat in a scheduling system that doesn't connect to the shop's email, texting tool or contacts.

### The analysis

We worked out with the team what each field meant, analyzed the records and reviewed the charts together.

### What changed

The analysis showed that volunteers 60 and older provide much of the shop's steady, long-term service. Within months, the team shifted recruiting toward them, and a nearby senior living community launched a monthly volunteer pilot.
