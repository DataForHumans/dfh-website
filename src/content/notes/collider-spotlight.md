Collider, the Rochester organization that supports local entrepreneurs, published a profile of Chris Roberts and Data for Humans on November 9. Megan Malugani's piece covers how a chemist came to build data tools for nonprofits, the homelessness dashboard we built for the Any Path Home coalition in Olmsted County, and Relayered Design, our 3D printing studio. Chris also talks about what Collider's CO.STARTERS program, coworking space and coaching connections meant for the business, and about Michael Soto joining as chief strategy officer this year.
